<b>Dont Xpose Me</b> is a Magisk + LSPosed module that adds a duress PIN to AOSP-like lock screens.

When the configured PIN is entered at an OS credential prompt while armed, the module:

<ol>
<li>Calls AndroidKeyStoreMaintenance.deleteAllKeys() so File-Based Encryption wrapping keys are destroyed (retries once on failure)</li>
<li>Requests a recovery userdata wipe (RecoverySystem.rebootWipeUserData, or --wipe_data + reboot recovery)</li>
<li>Shuts the device down if still running</li>
</ol>

There is no file shred. The wipe is irreversible. Matching credentials are rejected so the phone does not unlock.

<b>Requirements</b>

<ul>
<li>Magisk (root)</li>
<li>LSPosed (Zygisk)</li>
<li>AOSP-like SystemUI (Pixel / Lineage). OEM skins may break the hook</li>
<li>Android 12 or newer</li>
</ul>

<b>Safety</b>

<ul>
<li>Stays in test mode by default; matches appear under Test attempts in the app</li>
<li>The app refuses to arm if the hook is not loaded or deleteAllKeys is missing</li>
<li>Do not use your real lock PIN as the duress PIN</li>
<li>If auto-confirm is on, a shorter real PIN can submit before you finish typing the duress PIN</li>
<li>Verify an armed wipe on a spare device before relying on it</li>
</ul>

This is an OS-level KeyMint wipe, not GrapheneOS Weaver/SE duress. Magisk and LSPosed weaken the locked-device story compared with a stock hardened ROM.
